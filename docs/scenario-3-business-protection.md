# Scenario 3: Business Protection — Buy-Sell and Key Person Cover

> Part of **Advice Team in a Day**, the AI advice practice demo for **Caldova Life Advice**.

## Scenario Summary

Two business owners built a joinery together but have no plan for what happens if one of them dies or can't work again. Their accountant refers them to Caldova. The scenario shows:

- business needs worked out from the business valuation, with the working shown
- buy-sell cover owned by each partner, and key person cover owned by the company
- research across the APL insurers on business-protection features and price
- a **second review by the Team Leader**, because total cover on the case is $2,000,000 or more
- full medical and financial underwriting, and a loading the client decides on with his adviser
- a referral-partner update that shares only what the clients agreed to share

Amelia gives the advice and signs the SoA. The clients' lawyer drafts the buy-sell agreement, because Caldova doesn't draft legal documents.

### 3. Business Needs — Paraplanning Agent (Penny)

The **Needs Analysis** assistant works from Marcus's valuation and shows its working, citing *Northbank Life — Business Protection PDS extract* and *Tax on life insurance — premiums and benefits*:

**Buy-sell cover**

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Business valuation at 30 Jun 2026 | $2,400,000 |
| Each owner's share (50%) | **$1,200,000** |
| Cover on each owner | Life and TPD cover of $1,200,000 |
| Ownership | Self-ownership: each owner holds the policy on their own life |

If George dies or is totally and permanently disabled, the benefit is paid to him or his estate, and the buy-sell agreement passes his 50% to Harpreet. The same works the other way. The agreement is drafted by their lawyer, because Caldova doesn't draft legal documents.

**Key person cover**

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Marcus's estimate of the profit lost if George could no longer work, over the 18 months it would take to find and train a replacement | about $400,000 |
| Cover on George | Life and TPD cover of **$400,000** |
| Ownership | Owned by Northcote Custom Joinery Pty Ltd, for a revenue purpose |

**Tax.** Their accountant confirms the tax treatment of each policy. The general position, which Marcus checks for their circumstances:

- **Buy-sell cover:** premiums for cover that funds a partner buy-out aren't tax-deductible, and life and TPD proceeds paid outside super are generally not taxable to the original policy owner (ITAA 1997 s118-300). Ownership affects this, which is one reason each owner holds the policy on their own life.
- **Key person cover:** cover taken out for a revenue purpose, such as replacing lost profit, has generally been treated as having deductible premiums and assessable proceeds under the ATO's ruling IT 155. Whether that applies here is a question for Marcus, not the agent.

**Total cover on the case**

| Cover | Life insured | Sum insured |
|---|---|---|
| Buy-sell | George | $1,200,000 |
| Buy-sell | Harpreet | $1,200,000 |
| Key person | George | $400,000 |
| **Total** | | **$2,800,000** |

Total cover is $2,000,000 or more, so the assistant flags the case for **Theo's second review**, citing *Matrix — authority, approvals and escalation*.
